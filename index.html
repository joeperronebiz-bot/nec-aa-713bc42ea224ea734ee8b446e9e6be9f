While they decide

You can have it brought to NEC.

Your car stays secure and is evaluated at a licensed body shop, not a tow yard.

Your rights in Connecticut
  • You have the right to choose the licensed repair shop that fixes your car (CGS 38a-354).
  • You can’t be required to use the tow company for repairs (CT DMV Towing Bill of Rights).
